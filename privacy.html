Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

What this app is

Quran Horizons is a private automation tool built and operated by a single individual to publish pre-produced Quran recitation videos to one TikTok account, @thequranhorizons. It is not a public product, has no separate end users, and is not distributed to anyone else.

What data this app accesses

Through TikTok's Content Posting API, this app accesses only:

  • An OAuth access token scoped to publish videos on the @thequranhorizons account.
  • The video files, titles, and descriptions the operator supplies for that same account.

It does not read, collect, or process data belonging to any other TikTok user, viewer, or third party.

Where data is stored

The OAuth token is stored locally on the operator's own device and is used only to authorize publishing requests to TikTok's API. It is never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's own API endpoints, and never shared with a third party.

Data sharing

None. This app does not sell, rent, or share any data with advertisers, analytics services, or other third parties.

Your controls

As the account owner, access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's account settings under connected apps, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reach the operator via a direct message to @thequranhorizons on TikTok.
